#### Alumni Sports Program

### Alumni Golf Challenge

The flagship Sport Alumni Golf Challenge

![](../images/kenya-high-school-alumni.jpg)

What is The Alumni Golf Challenge?

The Alumni Golf Challenge is a golf series that brings together executives representing their alma mater to participate in a friendly competition. The victorious school is awarded a seed bursary fund, which can be further enhanced by alumni contributions, to support financially-disadvantaged students.

Additionally, various gifts are bestowed upon the outstanding teacher or the teacher with the longest tenure at the school. The event also includes contributions from corporate sponsors to enhance the school’s infrastructure.

The tagline is “Win for Your Alma Mater.

Eligibility criteria

- To participate in the tournament, individuals can either be alumnae of a participating school or choose to support another high school in need.
- Golfers must register for the tournament by paying a registration fee and agreeing to the tournament’s terms and conditions.
- Non-golfers have the option to sign up for attendance at the Awards ceremony.

How to apply

- Interested alumni can sign up for the tournament online or in-person, depending on the registration process set
- Corporate sponsors can also register to sponsor the tournament and provide funds for the programs.

#### Alumni Empowerment Program

### Mentorship & Development

Mentorship, Leadership Training & Career Development

![](../images/mentorship.jpg)

What is The Alumni Empowerment Program

These programs are designed to empower students from marginalized communities and equip them with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed academically and professionally. Additionally, Alumnus Challenge partners with schools and other organizations to develop and implement programs that address the specific needs of each community

Eligibility criteria

- Can be an alumnus of any school
- Must express interest and commitment to mentorship.
- May need to meet certain requirements as guided by the program.

How to apply

- Interested alumni can apply for the program by contacting us.
- Applicants may need to fill out an application form.
