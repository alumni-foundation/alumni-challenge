###### Message from the Chief Host

It started with a news article in 2020. The story read on the impending financial doom Kenyan schools faced, as a result of the Covid-19 pandemic. What started out as a desire to financially support learning institutions grew into a movement that challenges inequalities and ignites a culture of like-minded individuals, striving to make an impact in their alma maters and the broader Kenyan community.

When considering alumni, we tend to primarily associate them with fundraising. But alumni represent an untapped resource that goes far beyond what’s in their pocket – they represent hope. In a world plagued by a global pandemic, political turmoil, and rising cost of living, hope is a rare and valuable commodity. And yet, this is what the Alumni Challenge ultimately seeks to offer to those who may need it most in the education sector.

To do so, we believe that sports offer one of the greatest mediums to spread our message. It is not only an engaging activity that bridges diverse cultures and backgrounds, but it also invites the alumni to take pride in battling for the chance to win awards for their alma mater.

As Alumni Challenge, we have a dream of a Kenyan alumni movement made of a mosaic representation of all 47 counties, all working towards equal access to quality education. We believe this dream is not only possible, but that it holds great potential for sustainability, rippling through society to tangibly impact students, teachers, schools, families, and the communities surrounding them.

We invite you to join us in fighting for this dream.

### - Mark Macharia

CEO & Co-Founder

#### OUR PILLARS

### Pillars that guide our work and impact

### Student Empowerment

We believe in empowering students through the Alumni movement. We provide support and opportunities, including bursary aid and lifelong learning programs, to help students reach their full potential.

### Teacher Recognition

Through our Teacher Reward Pillar, we recognize and reward their hard work and dedication. We organize incentives and initiatives to acknowledge the positive impact they have on students' lives.

### Institutional Development

We are committed to the development of our school infrastructure. Through the Institutional Development Pillar, we mobilize alumni to contribute financially or through in-kind donations.

### Community Engagement

We understand the importance of giving back to the community. Our Community Engagement Pillar focuses on how alumni can make a positive impact on the surrounding area.

These pillars form the foundation of our work at Alumni Challenge, driving us to create lasting change in the lives of students, teachers, institutions, and communities.
