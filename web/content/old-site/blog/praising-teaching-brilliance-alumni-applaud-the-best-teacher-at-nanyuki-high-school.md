Mr. John Makato, the recipient of the prestigious Best **#Teacher** Award at the Nanyuki High School Alumni Homecoming. With a passion for education and a specialization in Computer Science, Mr. Makato has achieved remarkable success in his teaching career.

Born and raised in Nakuru county, Mr. Makato pursued his higher education majoring in Education with a focus on Computer Science and Mathematics. His pedagogic skills, combined with his dedication, have made him an outstanding educator.

Since joining Nanyuki School in 2016, Mr. Makato has been an **#inspiration** to both his students and colleagues. His commitment to instilling # values such as hard work, persistence, optimism, trustworthiness, and pushing beyond limits has created a positive learning environment.

Under Mr. Makato’s guidance, the Computer Science subject at **#Nanyuki** School has witnessed a remarkable improvement. In the last seven years, the mean grade for the subject has consistently risen, reaching its peak last year with a subject mean grade of 12—the highest in the school’s curriculum examination history. Notably, all his students achieved an ‘A’ grade in Computer Science.

This award of Best Teacher comes with valuable prizes that further recognize Mr. Makato’s exceptional contribution. Kenya Airways has generously contributed two return tickets to Zanzibar, while Steri Tours – Socially Responsible Travel has provided full-paid hotel accommodation, promising unforgettable memories for the fortunate recipients of the Best Teacher Award.
