Engineer Fabian Kebo, originally from **#Botswana** and currently working in **#kenya** , recently achieved a remarkable win at the highly anticipated 5th Edition of the Alumni Golf Challenge. Fabian’s journey began in the picturesque mountains of southern Botswana, where his passion for sports ignited. Over the years, he excelled in various disciplines like boxing and rugby league, but it was the game of golf that truly captured his heart. Through **#golf** , Fabian learned valuable virtues like discipline, integrity, and patience.

During his time in Kenya, Fabian became a member of the prestigious **#Nanyuki** Sports Club, where he found a vibrant community of fellow golf enthusiasts. Embracing the opportunity to explore Kenya’s diverse golf landscape, Fabian ventured out to twenty-two different golf clubs, forming connections and making a positive impact wherever he went. To express his gratitude for the warm hospitality he received at the Nanyuki Sports Club, Fabian sponsored a local golf competition called Eng. Fabian Golf Day.

However, Fabian’s golfing journey reached its pinnacle at the highly competitive Alumni Golf Challenge. This event brings together alumni from all walks of life, competing not only for personal glory but also for prizes that benefit their former schools. The top prize includes an education bursary fund, a School Makeover package, and Holiday packages for the best-performing teachers. As an international player, Fabian chose to represent a local high school, **#Nanyuki** Boys High, which is located near his workplace. Beyond anyone’s expectations, Fabian emerged victorious, bringing immense joy and fulfillment to himself and the entire Nanyuki Boys High School community.

This triumph goes far beyond individual achievement. It serves as an inspiration, reminding us of the incredible potential that lies within the corridors of Nanyuki Boys High School. By winning on their behalf, Fabian has not only brought honor to the school’s name but has also united people across nations.

The Alumni Golf Challenge is organized by the Alumni Challenge, an non for profit initiative committed to fostering an **#alumni** movement in the field of **#education**. Their noble initiative aims to address educational disparities faced by marginalized **#students** , ensuring equal opportunities for all. Participants in the challenge have the chance to compete for the top prizes, fostering excellence, camaraderie, and philanthropy among alumni from various backgrounds.
