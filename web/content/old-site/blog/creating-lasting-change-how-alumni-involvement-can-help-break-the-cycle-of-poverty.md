Hannah Mumbi Ngigi is a determined Form 2 student at Ndumberi Girls’ Secondary School. Despite coming from a **#financially** struggling family, Hannah has big dreams of becoming a doctor and helping not only her family but also others in similar situations. With the help of her primary school headteacher, Hannah managed to secure a place in Ndumberi Girls’ School, where she has been working hard to achieve her academic goals.

However, financial constraints have continued to be a hurdle for Hannah, and she has been sent home on several occasions due to lack of school fees. Fortunately, her situation took a turn for the better when an alumna of Ndumberi Girls’ School, Magdalene Mbugua, won the third edition of the **#Alumni** Golf Challenge. Magdalene’s victory did not only earn her recognition and prizes but also provided an **#opportunity** to give back to her former school by donating a bursary to a deserving student like Hannah.

The Alumni Golf Challenge is an excellent initiative that brings together alumni from different schools to raise funds for their alma maters. Through this initiative, alumni get a chance to connect and network while also giving back to their communities in a meaningful way. Magdalene’s generous donation has not only enabled Hannah to continue with her studies but has also inspired her to work even harder towards achieving her dreams.

Hannah’s story is a testament to the importance of alumni involvement in their former schools. By supporting academic programs, providing mentorship, and participating in social initiatives, alumni can make a significant difference in the lives of current students. Hannah’s determination to succeed despite the challenges she faces is a shining example of the resilience and grit that characterizes many students from disadvantaged backgrounds. With the right support, such students can achieve their dreams and become valuable members of society.

If you’re an alumnus or alumna of any school, this story should inspire you to reach out and support your former school. Your school engagement and contribution can make a significant impact and create a better future for the next generation of students.
