**Nanyuki, Kenya** – On Friday, 23rd June 2023, the lively town of Nanyuki, well-known for its equator point, buzzed with excitement as the 5th Alumni **#Homecoming** unfolded at Nanyuki High School. Organized by Alumni Challenge, an organization dedicated to fostering connections among alumni, the event aimed to celebrate the triumph of Fabian Kebo (5th Alumni Golf Challenge winner) and hand over his well-deserved prizes to his **#Alma** mater.

The day commenced with an atmosphere of anticipation as more than a thousand students eagerly congregated in the plenary hall to welcome their **#Champion**. Distinguished guests, including Dr. Moses Ikiara, Chairman of the School Board, and other members of the school’s executive team, graced the event. The festivities commenced with a captivating performance by the National Award-winning Choir, who enchanted the audience with nostalgic renditions of beloved folk songs, “Zilizopendwa and Joysi.”

In his warm welcome speech, Mr. Solomon Koech, the School **#Principal**, set the stage for a memorable event. Mr. Albert Wanjau, Chairman of the **#Alumni** executive of Nanyuki School, took the opportunity to reflect on the school’s history and its remarkable growth since his time as a student. Mark Macharia, the CEO of Alumni Challenge, was introduced to the audience, emphasizing the significance of the event and expressing gratitude to the partners and sponsors who made it all possible.

The highlight of the day arrived with an inspiring address by the guest of honor, Corporal Fabian Kebo, dressed immaculately in his official military uniform. With passion and conviction, Fabian shared a powerful message with the **#students**, emphasizing the invaluable life lessons that **#sports** teach, such as self-discipline, determination, and a healthy lifestyle. His words resonated deeply, leaving a lasting impact on all those in attendance.

Following Fabian’s speech, the much-anticipated award ceremony commenced. Master Michael Ekaale, a Form 1 student, received a **#bursary** worth one hundred thousand shillings, providing essential support for his educational journey. Additionally, Mr. John Makato, a computer teacher, received the prestigious Best Teacher Award, recognizing his exceptional commitment to his profession.

The generosity of the sponsors was evident as Nanyuki High School received a variety of valuable prizes. Kenya Airways contributed two return tickets to Zanzibar, and Steri Tours – Socially Responsible Travel provided full-paid hotel accommodation, promising unforgettable memories for the fortunate recipients for the best teacher. Crown Paints Kenya Plc generously donated products enhancing the school’s aesthetics and creating an inspiring learning environment.

The spirit of empowerment continued with two students being selected for a one-week apprenticeship in Digital Marketing, Production, and Photography, courtesy of Socialmeds Digital Limited . This hands-on experience will equip the chosen students with sought-after skills in these fields.

Nanyuki High School’s commitment to providing a comprehensive education was further supported by the provision of one term of newspaper supply courtesy of Nation Media Group .This initiative will keep students informed and engaged with current events, enriching their learning experience. Additionally, waridi eyewear donated three pairs of spectacle frames, contributing to the well-being and visual health of deserving individuals, improving their overall quality of life.

Furthermore, ROHTO Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. contributed by distributing branded sportswear to Nanyuki High School, greatly to benefit students during sports activities.

That’s not all! In collaboration with the Alumni Challenge, Unicaf also stepped forward to offer an incredible opportunity of 80% scholarship for undergraduate and postgraduate degrees with Unicaf‘s esteemed partner universities in the UK and Africa. This scholarship program opens doors for students to pursue their higher education dreams and unlock a world of possibilities.

The event culminated with a vote of thanks from Mr. Peter Ichaara, a teacher at Nanyuki School, expressing gratitude to all those involved in making the day a resounding success.
